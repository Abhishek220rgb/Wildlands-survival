SURVIVAL

A mobile 3D wilderness prototype. Explore a large procedural landscape, discover roaming wildlife, gather supplies and test the hunting system.

World: 1.2 km × 1.2 km prototype map
Wildlife: 20 species types, with local spawning
Controls: touch joystick + drag to look

Deer • Elk • Moose • Bison • Boar • Wolf • Fox • Bear • Rabbit • Hare • Coyote • Lynx • Mountain goat • Sheep • Zebra • Giraffe • Elephant • Crocodile • Ostrich • Eagle

This first version uses simple low-poly placeholder animals. We can replace them with detailed 3D models and add richer animal behaviour in later versions.
